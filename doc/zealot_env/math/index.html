Minimal vector / quaternion helpers.

zealot-env builds with zero external dependencies (so it compiles without the cargo-gpu toolchain), so we can’t lean on glam/nalgebra here. These are the handful of operations the MDP needs: rotating a world vector into the base frame and back, and reading a body axis out of a quaternion. Quaternions are (x, y, z, w) to match nexus’s Rotation readback.

Rotate vector v by unit quaternion q = (x, y, z, w) (body→world if q is the body’s orientation). Uses v' = v + 2·q_xyz × (q_xyz × v + w·v).
