Expand description

Per-foot state needed by the contact-shaped rewards. The env fills this from the simulator’s contacts; air_time is tracked across steps by the env.

Fields§

§contact: bool

Foot is touching the ground this step.

§first_contact: bool

Touchdown this step (was airborne last step, now in contact).

§air_time: f32

Seconds the foot has been airborne (0 while in contact).

§height: f32

Foot world height, m.

§planar_speed: f32

Foot horizontal speed, m/s (for slip / clearance shaping).

§tilt: f32

Sole tilt from horizontal, rad (0 = sole flat on the ground). Used by the flat-foot reward so the robot plants its whole sole, not an edge/toe/heel.

§yaw_rel_base: f32

Foot yaw RELATIVE to the base (rad; 0 = foot points the same direction as base). Computed by the env as atan2(y, x) of q_base⁻¹·q_foot · X̂. Used by feet_yaw_mean.

§pos_xy: [f32; 2]

Foot world horizontal position (m). The reward uses the difference between the two feet’s positions, transformed into the base frame, to compute the lateral stance width.

§alt_step: bool

Touchdown this step that ALTERNATED feet — first_contact AND the other foot was the most recent to touch down (set by the env, which tracks the last-touchdown foot per env). The swing/air-time reward keys off this so a step only pays when feet alternate (L→R→L→R): a foot held permanently in the air never touches down (no reward), and double-tapping the same foot (hopping) earns nothing on the repeat. Forces a real alternating gait.

§vz: f32

Foot vertical velocity, m/s (world +Z; negative = descending). Env finite-diff, 0 on the first step after reset. Drives the touchdown_vz slam penalty — the KINEMATIC impact term (a slam is a foot still descending fast near the ground; kinematics transfer across engines far better than contact-sensor forces do).

§force_rate: f32

One-step change in this foot’s sensed normal contact force, in BODY WEIGHTS per control step (|F_t − F_{t−1}| / (m·g), ≥ 0). Filled by the env from the contact-force sensor (BIPED_CONTACT_SENSE); 0 when force sensing is off or on the first step after a reset. Drives the force_rate ground-reaction-smoothness penalty: a hard touchdown is a 1–2 BW jump in one step, and the standing tremor is a sustained left/right load dither — both are pure force-rate, while calm standing and normal gait weight transfer (~0.06–0.12 BW/step) are ~free.
