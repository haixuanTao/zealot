Expand description

Reward term weights (per-second; the task scales by the control dt). Defaults are the deployed policy’s. deferred_* weights are recorded for parity but their terms aren’t summed yet (they need contact readback).

Fields§

§track_lin_vel: f32

Linear-velocity tracking (exp kernel), std below.

§forward_progress: f32

Linear forward-progress reward: w · clamp(v·ĉmd, 0, |cmd|). A non-saturating gradient toward the commanded direction (breaks the march-in-place dead zone where the exp tracking kernel is flat). Folded into the track_lin_vel term.

§track_ang_vel: f32

Angular (yaw) velocity tracking (exp kernel).

§upright: f32

Upright / flat-orientation (exp kernel on tilt).

§base_height: f32

Base-height tracking (exp kernel) — keeps the robot standing tall instead of crouching to trivially avoid falling.

§base_height_target: f32

Target base (torso) height, m (for base_height) while MOVING.

§base_height_target_stand: f32

Target base height, m, while STANDING (cmd.speed() < 0.1, the same gate the gait clock freezes on). Defaults to base_height_target, so the split is inert until BIPED_BASE_HEIGHT_STAND is set.

Standing tall is nearly free in height but expensive in knee angle: measured on this robot, height = 0.580*cos(knee/2) + 0.259, so the leg ceiling is 0.839 m at knee 0 and the curve goes FLAT as it straightens (15 deg -> 0 deg of knee buys only 5 mm). Two consequences:

  • a target ABOVE ~0.839 is unreachable without hyperextending into the -0.087 rad knee stop. That is what v22 did at 0.841 (locked at -4 deg, 60% of frames on the stop, CoT 1.00) – do not set one.
  • above ~0.837 the gradient falls under 0.5 mm/deg, so the only way to chase the last millimetres is to ride the stop. 0.835 -> ~13.6 deg knee with 18.6 deg of stop margin, 4 mm under the ceiling: reachable at a legal knee angle, unlike v22’s target.
§pose: f32

Hip yaw/roll deviation penalty gain (NEGATIVE). L2 penalty on hipz+hipx deviation from default, always-on — stops the policy from limit-riding the lateral hips into a splayed brace. (Was an unused full-posture reward.)

§bilateral_symmetry: f32

Left/right symmetry (exp kernel on mirror error).

§action_rate: f32

L2 penalty on action change.

§action_rate_hipz_hipx: f32

L2 penalty on action change for the hip yaw/roll DOFs only.

§body_ang_vel: f32

L2 penalty on base roll/pitch angular velocity.

§lin_vel_z: f32

L2 penalty on vertical base velocity.

§dof_pos_limits: f32

Soft joint-position-limit penalty.

§dof_vel: f32

L2 penalty on joint velocity.

§termination: f32

One-shot penalty applied by the env on a non-timeout termination.

§air_time: f32

Feet air-time bonus on touchdown (shapes a stepping gait).

§flight: f32

Penalty applied every step BOTH feet are off the ground. Forces a walking gait (always ≥1 foot planted) instead of hopping/bounding.

§single_support: f32

Bonus applied (while moving) every step EXACTLY ONE foot is on the ground. Single-support is the defining phase of walking — rewarding it directly makes stepping beat both standing (double-support) and hopping (flight).

§stand_planted: f32

Penalty (per airborne foot, per step) while the command is STANDING. Makes the policy absorb small pushes with ankle/hip torque (feet planted, CoM shifted) instead of dance-stepping in place. MODERATE by design: for a big shove a protective step (brief penalty) must stay cheaper than falling (termination), mirroring the human ankle→hip→step strategy ladder. 0 = off.

§foot_slip: f32

Foot-slip penalty (horizontal foot speed while in contact).

§action_rate_rate: f32

Action second-difference penalty (NEGATIVE): Σ(a − 2a′ + a″)². The ACTION-side tremor term — high-frequency dither is literally an oscillating action, so this prices it at the policy’s output with no engine-specific signal in the loop. Complements (does not stack with) action_rate, which only prices speed of change, not oscillation: a smooth fast ramp scores high action_rate but ~0 here. 0 = off.

§touchdown_vz: f32

Touchdown-impact penalty (NEGATIVE): per airborne foot below touchdown_vz_h (m), squared excess descent speed beyond touchdown_vz_ok (m/s). Kinematic slam term: a soft landing decel- erates BEFORE the ground, a slam is still fast at 5 cm. 0 = off.

§touchdown_vz_ok: f32

Allowed descent speed at the gate height (m/s, default 0.3).

§touchdown_vz_h: f32

Gate height above local ground (m, default 0.10). NOTE this compares against FootObs.height, which is the foot link ORIGIN — ~0.035 m above the sole even when planted (FOOT_REST_H). The first default (0.06) left a ~2 cm trigger window that contact sensing pre-empted: the term logged exactly 0 for 100 iters of newborn flailing. 0.10 = sole ~6.5 cm up, a real approach window.

§force_rate: f32

Ground-reaction smoothness penalty (NEGATIVE): per foot, the one-step sensed-force change above force_rate_deadband (both in body weights per control step), squared. Prices the two behaviours v26 shipped with: foot-slam touchdowns (measured 0.9 BW mean / 2.1 BW peak first-contact jumps in MuJoCo) and the standing tremor (left/right load share dithering 0→1 at ~18 Hz with both feet planted — invisible in base motion, but exactly a sustained |ΔF|). Deadband keeps normal gait weight transfer (~0.06–0.12 BW/step) free, so walking itself is not taxed. 0 = off. Needs BIPED_CONTACT_SENSE (no sensor → term is 0).

§force_rate_deadband: f32

Deadband for force_rate, body weights per control step (default 0.15).

§foot_clearance: f32

Foot-clearance penalty (swing-foot height vs target).

§foot_clearance_target: f32

Target swing-foot clearance height, m (for foot_clearance).

§foot_orientation: f32

Flat-foot penalty: penalizes sole tilt (rad²) of a foot IN CONTACT, so the robot plants its whole sole flat instead of balancing on a toe/heel/edge.

§feet_yaw_mean: f32

WBC’s feet_yaw_mean_vs_base: penalty on each foot’s yaw (in base frame), summed over both feet. Strong because un-shaped foot yaw is the dominant posture artefact for under-constrained humanoid RL.

§feet_yaw_diff: f32

WBC’s feet_yaw_diff_l2: penalty on the squared wrapped yaw difference between the two feet (splayed/pigeon-toed stance). AGILE G1 weight −0.1, lerobot −0.02.

§feet_distance: f32

WBC’s feet_distance_from_ref (lateral mode): penalises deviation of the lateral (body-Y) foot separation from feet_distance_ref.

§feet_distance_ref: f32

Reference lateral foot separation, m (for feet_distance).

§gait_clock: f32

Periodic gait-clock reward (dense). Each step, each foot earns up to this weight for matching its prescribed phase: airborne during its swing window, in contact during its stance window. Provides the dense gradient toward an alternating gait that the sparse touchdown bonus could not.

§gait_swing_ratio: f32

Fraction of each foot’s gait cycle spent in swing (rest is stance). With the feet offset by half a cycle, 1 - 2·swing_ratio of the cycle is double-support — the built-in “both feet down in the middle”.

pub fn agile() -> Self

WBC-AGILE’s G1 velocity_history reward set, term for term (BIPED_AGILE_REWARDS=1). AGILE has NO stepping rewards — locomotion emerges from sharp tracking + terrain — so every zealot-only shaping term (gait_clock, air_time, single_support, bilateral_symmetry, pose, forward_progress) is zeroed here. Terms AGILE has that we can’t express (action_rate_rate, root_acc, dof_vel_limits, torque family — those live in the env’s BIPED_TORQUE_W/POWER_W hooks) are noted at the call site. flight carries AGILE’s jumping weight.
