PPO with a diagonal-Gaussian actor-critic — the rsl_rl tier.

Matches the deployed policy’s training config: separate actor/critic MLPs (ELU, e.g. [512, 256, 128]), a state-independent learnable log_std, GAE(λ) advantages, the clipped surrogate over K epochs × M minibatches, an adaptive-KL learning rate, an entropy bonus, clipped value loss, and global grad-norm clipping.

The actor sees the (noised, partial) policy observation; the critic sees the privileged observation — the asymmetric setup AGILE uses. The update is a direct, allocation-light port of the math in pendulum_ppo, generalized to many action dims and a real value function.
